## What counts as commercial use

Examples of use that requires a commercial license:

- Embedding or self-hosting the simulations in a paid product, a
  subscription platform, a freemium app, or a service that generates
  revenue (including through advertising).
- Adapting, translating, re-skinning, or bundling the simulations for
  sale or as part of a sold offering.
- Use by a for-profit company for its customers, employees, or
  marketing.
- Offering the simulations, modified or not, as part of paid
  consulting, tutoring, or courseware.

Examples of use that stays free under the public license:

- A teacher using or modifying a sim in class, including at a private
  school, so long as the school itself is an educational institution.
- A student, researcher, or hobbyist studying or extending the code.
- A non-profit, charity, or government body hosting the sims for the
  public.

If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask. A quick
email costs nothing.

## How to obtain a commercial license

Email **listyantidewi@gmail.com** with the subject line
`Commercial license: All-Science Sims` and include:

1. Who you are (company name, website, country of registration).
2. Which simulations you want to use (names from the
   [catalog](README.md#catalog)), or whether you want the whole
   collection.
3. How you will use them (hosting, adaptation, translation,
   distribution channel, paid or free tier).
4. The size of the audience (current and expected users or seats)
   and the pricing of the product the sims will be part of.

You will receive a quote and a draft agreement. Pricing is set per
request, based on the number of simulations, the scope of use, and the
size of the audience.

## Standard commercial terms

Every commercial license is **non-exclusive**: the copyright holder
remains free to license the same simulations to others and to keep
publishing them here. Beyond that, a typical agreement includes:

- **Scope**: the named simulations, for the named product or platform,
  including the right to self-host, adapt the interface, and translate.
- **Term and fee**: usually an annual fee, renewable, with the license
  ending if payment stops. One-time or revenue-share arrangements can
  be discussed.
- **Attribution**: a visible credit to All-Science Sims with a link to
  this repository on or near each adapted simulation, and the required
  copyright notice retained in the source.
- **No sublicensing**: the licensee may not pass the simulations on to
  third parties under their own license.
- **Grant-back**: translations, bug fixes, and improvements made to the
  licensed simulations may be merged back into this project under its
  public license, so the free version keeps improving.
- **No implied endorsement**: the licensee may not use the copyright
  holder's name to suggest endorsement of their product without
  separate permission.
- **Ownership**: the copyright holder keeps all rights in the original
  simulations; the licensee owns only its own original additions.

These are starting points, not a take-it-or-leave-it list. The final
agreement is negotiated per licensee.
