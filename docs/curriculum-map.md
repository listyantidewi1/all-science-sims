# Curriculum map

Every simulation carries a suggested grade range (`grade` in its manifest; 1–12 = school year, 13 = university). The site filters by level on the home and subject pages, and `#/<subject>/<sim>` pages show the range next to the title. This file lists the collection by level with the closest standards families, as a starting point for lesson planning. Frameworks: NGSS (US), the national curriculum for England (KS1–KS4), and Kurikulum Merdeka (Indonesia, Fase A–F).
